THE CHALLENGE WE SOLVE

Students Have Skills. But Is Their Career Journey Organized?

Traditional student career tracking is fragmented and inefficient. CareerSync fixes all 4 major hurdles:

01 · Scattered Information

Scattered Information

Resume details, technical skills, certifications, and GitHub projects spread across multiple unstructured platforms and files.

02 · Hidden Skill Gaps

Hidden Skill Gaps

No structured or automated way to identify what technical skills are missing for target industry roles like Full-Stack, AI/ML, or Data Science.

03 · Manual Management

Manual Management

Achievements, certificates, and resumes maintained manually without a consistent, ATS-optimized professional format.

04 · No Progress Tracking

No Progress Tracking

Career growth, milestone completion, and preparation readiness are difficult to monitor and benchmark over time.
